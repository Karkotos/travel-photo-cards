清水寺No.04 — 10
The stage at 7:40
The wooden stage is still asleep; the queue at Otowa waterfall is already longer than the stream. One early hour buys you the whole valley in silence — and a photo spot with nobody in it.
07:40 · the stageD1 · Higashiyama
八坂通No.05 — 10
Uphill, then the pagoda
Shops along the Sannenzaka slopes are still shuttered; only the lanterns are awake. At the top of the stone paving you look up and the pagoda stands exactly in the middle of the street — Kyoto's best-composed block.
The pagoda ends the street, on purpose.
08:20 · Yasaka-doriD1 · Higashiyama
Gion,
at dusk
Through the big trees of Maruyama Park and into Hanamikoji as the lanterns come on. A shamisen is being tuned behind a door; everyone in the lane lowers their voice.

鸭川No.07 — 10
Reach the river
as it turns dark
Pontocho lights up one lantern at a time; on the banks someone runs, someone plays guitar, someone does nothing at all. A Kyoto day should end here — sit until the wind cools.

Arashiyama
Bamboo
before
the crowd
At 7:30 the grove holds only wind and the dry rattle of leaves; the sightseeing train has not started. Walk ten more minutes past the Tenryu-ji gate and the path empties out completely.

伏见稻荷No.09 — 10
A thousand gates,
keep climbing
Visitors queue to photograph the gates at the base; fewer than one in ten reach Yotsutsuji halfway up. The view over southern Kyoto from that terrace repays every step — and the mountain, at least, charges no entry.
Climb past the crowd line.
